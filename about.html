ABOUT DISASTER RELIEF

Helping communities respond, recover and rebuild.

DISASTER RELIEF is a digital platform designed to connect people affected by disasters with volunteers, relief teams and administrators.

During floods, cyclones, earthquakes, landslides, fires and other emergencies, quick communication and organized support can save lives. Our platform provides a central place where citizens can request help, volunteers can offer assistance and administrators can coordinate relief activities.

DISASTER TYPES & PREPAREDNESS

Know the disaster. Know the action.

🌊

Flood

Heavy rain, river overflow, flash floods

Before Keep emergency kit, identify higher ground, protect important documents
During Move to higher ground, avoid floodwater, follow official warnings
After Avoid contaminated water and return only when authorities say it is safe
🌪️

Cyclone

Strong winds, storm surge

Before Secure doors and windows, store food and water, charge phones
During Stay indoors away from windows and follow official alerts
After Watch for damaged buildings, fallen wires and flooding
🌎

Earthquake

Ground shaking

Before Secure heavy furniture and prepare an emergency kit
During Drop, Cover, Hold On and stay away from windows
After Check injuries, gas leaks and structural damage; expect aftershocks
🔥

Fire

Building, forest, electrical fire

Before Install smoke alarms, keep exits clear and learn evacuation routes
During Raise the alarm, evacuate, stay low in smoke and call emergency services
After Do not re-enter until authorities declare it safe
⛈️

Thunderstorm / Lightning

Lightning, strong winds

Before Monitor weather warnings and identify the safest shelter
During Go indoors, stay away from windows and electrical equipment
After Avoid damaged power lines and report hazards immediately
🌋

Landslide

Heavy rain, unstable slopes

Before Know landslide-prone areas and monitor warnings
During Move away from slopes and drainage channels when warned
After Stay away from unstable areas and damaged roads
☀️

Heatwave

Extreme temperatures

Before Stay hydrated and plan outdoor activities carefully
During Stay in a cool place, drink water and avoid strenuous activity
After Watch for heat illness and seek medical help when needed
🌊

Tsunami

Undersea earthquake

Before Know evacuation routes and warning signs
During If near the coast, move quickly to higher ground or inland
After Stay away from the coast until authorities give an all-clear
EMERGENCY KIT

Be ready before disaster hits.

  • 💧 Drinking water
  • 🥫 Ready-to-eat food
  • 🔦 Torch
  • 🔋 Power bank
  • 📻 Battery or hand-crank radio
  • 🩹 First-aid kit
  • 💊 Essential medicines
  • 😷 Masks
  • 📄 Copies of important documents
  • 🧤 Basic protective clothing
  • 💰 Some emergency cash
  • 📱 Charged mobile phone
BASIC RULE

BEFORE → PREPARE

Know the risks, make a family emergency plan, save emergency contacts and prepare supplies.

DURING → PROTECT

Stay calm, follow official instructions, move to a safe location and avoid unnecessary risks.

AFTER → CHECK & HELP

Check for injuries, avoid damaged structures and utilities, use safe water and food, and help others when it is safe.
