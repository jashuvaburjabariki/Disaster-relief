Safety guidelines

  • Wear protective equipment and carry identification at all times.
  • Check in with the Mandal Officer before starting work and record field status.
  • Never enter unsafe floodwaters or structural collapse zones without trained support.
  • Keep emergency helpline 112 and Mandal Officer direct contacts on rapid dial.
  • Photograph field situation and relief distribution before filing completion report.

MANDAL OFFICER DIRECTIVES & ACTION INSTRUCTIONS:
1. Immediate On-Site Assessment & Verification:

Proceed to the incident coordinates safely. Verify citizen identity, evaluate structural hazards, flood levels, and immediate danger to human lives.

2. Humanitarian Assistance Coordination:

Assess emergency relief needs (food rations, drinking water packets, blankets, medical first aid, shelter). If affected people require supplies, use the Assistance Coordination section to notify the Mandal Office immediately.

3. Photographic Evidence & Record Keeping:

Take clear photos of the affected site, damages, and relief distribution. High-quality photographic evidence is strictly required before submission for Mandal Officer verification.

4. Officer Verification & Sign-off:

Upon executing relief measures, submit the complete task dossier and photo evidence to the Mandal Officer for final review and approval.
